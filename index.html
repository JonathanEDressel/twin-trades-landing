Process

Four steps to hands-free investing

Set it up once and never think about it again — from brokerage connection to fully automated trades.

01

Connect Your Brokerage

Link Webull, Alpaca, or Charles Schwab via secure OAuth. Credentials are AES-128 encrypted and never stored in plain text.

02

Choose Who to Follow

Browse politicians, insiders, and verified traders ranked by returns and risk. Subscribe to those who match your strategy.

03

Trades Mirror Instantly

When a followed trader places a position, Twin Trades rebalances your portfolio proportionally and automatically.

04

Track Your Returns

Monitor live P&L, full trade history, and receive monthly performance reports delivered to your inbox.
